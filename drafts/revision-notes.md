# Revision notes

A polish pass on the kiln text. The scene, its order and its last line are unchanged.

## Tightened image

- The tick. "a small clear tick: a glaze settling onto a bowl" became "one small clear
  tick: glaze cooling on a bowl". Glaze does not settle onto a pot in a cooling kiln, it
  is already there; it ticks because it is cooling. "one" makes it a single sound in the
  quiet, and the shorter phrase lands on two long vowels (cool, bowl).

## Cleverness dropped

- "That cannot be hurried, and the potter knows it." read as the text's moral, stated.
  Now "The potter does not wait up.": the same patience, shown by what she does.
- "The bowls are already what they are going to be." was an aphorism, the one line that
  asked to be admired. Now "The firing is over.", a plain fact that carries the same rest.
- The cat "folded herself into" the warmest place: a cute turn. Now she "is asleep by the
  kiln door, where the floor is warm", which also ties her to the kiln's heat.

## Kept

- "No one needs to see them yet." stays as the closing line: it is the thought to return to.
- No "you", no instructions, no wellness words.

## Length

569 code points (was 632), limit 1,000. Shorter only because the cut words were not needed.
