# Candidates

Judge's order: (1) relaxes, (2) shown not instructed, (3) craft, nothing cuttable,
(4) lingers, (5) fresh. No imperatives, no "you", no stock wellness words.

## A. Kiln

The kiln was shut off at noon.

It will be morning before it is cool enough to open, and nothing anyone does can bring that sooner. The potter knows this. She has swept the floor, hung her apron on its nail, and gone up to bed.

So the shed is left to itself. The bricks give back their heat a little at a time. Now and then, from inside, comes a small clear tick: a glaze settling onto a bowl. Then quiet again.

Outside, frost comes down over the field. Inside, the warmth is slow to leave. The cat has found the place on the floor where it is best, and folded herself there.

The bowls are already what they are going to be. They sit in the dark in a row, holding their warmth, done.

No one needs to see them yet.

Critique: 1 strong: the waiting is enforced and accepted, nothing is asked. 2 pure showing.
3 short plain sentences, long vowels (slow, cool, noon, bowl). 4 the finished bowls in the warm
dark, unseen. 5 I have not seen a kiln in a calm text. Best so far.

## B. Dough

The dough is under its cloth on the shelf above the stove. No one is watching it. It rises anyway, at the speed dough rises, which is the speed of the evening. The baker has gone to sit on the step. Swifts are turning over the yard. When she comes back in, the cloth will be a little rounder than she left it.

Critique: 1 good. 3 "at the speed dough rises" is a bit pleased with itself. 4 the rounder cloth
is a nice image but small. 5 bread is a familiar cosy prop. Weaker than A on 4 and 5.

## C. Ferry

The last ferry is tied up for the night. The rope goes slack, then firm, then slack, as the water
lifts the hull and sets it down. The ticket window is dark. A gull stands on the rail with one foot
drawn up. The river goes on to the sea without taking anyone.

Critique: 1 good, rocking rhythm. 5 water and boats are close to stock. The last line is good but
slightly melancholy, which is not the same as at ease. Loses to A on 1 and 5.

## D. Direct note to the judge

There is no wrong answer here, and nobody is waiting on you...

Critique: allowed by the rules, but it tells rather than shows (fails 2) and reminds the reader
of the judging, which raises tension (hurts 1). Rejected.

## Pick

A, the kiln. Borrow from C the idea of a rhythm that repeats (tick, then quiet).

## Revision after re-reading A as the judge

- "nothing anyone does can bring that sooner" -> "That cannot be hurried, and the potter knows it."
- dropped "So" before "the shed is left to itself"
- dropped "Inside, the warmth is slow to leave" (the bricks line already says it)
- cat: "found the warmest place on the floor and folded herself into it"
- "holding their warmth, done" -> "still warm" (softer last sound before the closing line)
